The end-to-end record

One record, every stage of the property's life.

Each stage writes to the same controlled record, so allocation, costing, payments and title never drift apart across departments.

1

Land registered

Parcel captured, approved and marked available for a project or sale.

2

Project & plan

Master plan and units defined against the land parcel.

3

Costed & priced

Costs allocated to units; approved selling and rental prices set.

4

Finished inventory

WIP at 100% converts to available apartments, plots and spaces.

5

Reserved & sold

Reservation lock, KYC, agreement and payment to ownership.

6

Title issued

Deed processed and tracked through e-Ardhi against a clean record.

11 modules · 124 requirements

Eleven modules, one system of record.

Each module owns its domain and writes to a shared record — no re-keying between land, finance, sales and leasing.

01
LM

Land Management

Register parcels, track status and acquisition history, and control allocation to projects or sale.

02
PDI

Project Development & Investment

Create projects and master plans, define units, and manage phases against a land parcel.

03
CFE

Costing & Financial Engine

Allocate cost to units, apply versioned pricing rules, and run profit and margin analysis.

04
FPI

Finished Product & Inventory

Convert completed WIP into inventory with reservation locks and real-time availability.

05
IPV

Investment Property Valuation

Register REIT assets, run periodic valuation, and keep an immutable history for NAV.

06
BIL

Billing Management

Configure service charges, issue unified bills with GePG control numbers, and reconcile payments.

07
HLM

House Lease Management

Booking, KYC, agreements, rent cycles, renewals and tenant handover, end to end.

08
HSM

House Sales Management

Reservation, buyer qualification, cash / mortgage / hire-purchase, and title processing.

09
PSM

Plot Sales Management

Surveyed-plot reservation, eligibility, instalment plans, and e-Ardhi title transfer.

10
RAN

Reporting & Analytics

Operational and executive reports on portfolio, revenue, profitability and performance.

11
UAA

Configuration & Access

Users, roles and RBAC permissions, system parameters and configurable business rules.

12
CM

Customer Management

A single client identity for buyers and tenants across every transaction and record.
